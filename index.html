01

analog-neuron

Arduino · Python

A neuron's weighted sum done by resistors instead of a processor. Inputs are voltages, weights are conductances, and Kirchhoff's current law does the multiply-accumulate. Stage 1 is built and measured on a breadboard; stage 2 (weighted inputs, bias, op-amp activation) is designed and its firmware compiled.

Stage 1 error vs. ideal≤ 2.3 ADC steps
Weight mismatch~0.4%
Repeatabilitywithin 1 step over 7 cycles

dp4a-unit

Verilog

A Verilog DP4A: four int8 multiplies summed into a 32-bit accumulator in one operation, the instruction NVIDIA added to Pascal GPUs. One 9×9 signed multiplier covers every signed/unsigned mode, and it computes layer 1 of the MNIST network above.

int8-rollout-engine

Verilog · Python · CUDA

Sampling-based model-predictive control with the rollouts in hardware. Every control tick, 32 int8 lanes imagine 32 futures of a pendulum through a small learned physics model, score them, and return the best plan. An 8-lane build runs on a real Tang Nano 20K FPGA, choosing every action in a hardware-in-the-loop closed loop.
